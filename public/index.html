Our Story
Sanchita's Cake Corner
Est. 2015 · Ekbarna, Malda · Baked with Love

Every great story begins with a single step — ours began with a single cake. Back in 2015, in the quiet lanes of Ekbarna, Malda, a young woman named Sanchita Misra made a decision that would forever change the way her town celebrated its sweetest moments.

"If it's made with love, it will always taste like home."

What started as a humble passion — baking late into the night, experimenting with Belgian chocolate, perfecting every layer of sponge — quietly grew into something far greater than Sanchita had ever imagined. Word spread from neighbour to neighbour, from one birthday table to the next, from one wedding celebration to another. People didn't just come for the cake. They came for the feeling.

Over 10 wonderful years, Sanchita's Cake Corner has blossomed from a home kitchen dream into a beloved bakery — a place where stone-baked pizzas emerge golden and hot, where hand-crafted cookies fill the air with warmth, and where every custom cake tells a story as unique as the person it is made for.

10Years of Baking
∞Happy Customers
100%Made with Love

Today, from our little corner of Malda, we serve birthday cakes that make children's eyes light up, wedding cakes that bring tears of joy, and everyday treats that turn ordinary evenings into something special. Every item that leaves our kitchen carries with it a piece of Sanchita's heart — her dedication, her warmth, and her unwavering belief that food, when made with love, has the power to bring people together.

We are more than a bakery. We are a family — and you are always welcome at our table.

✦
Sanchita Misra
Founder & Head Baker · Sanchita's Cake Corner

Our Return Policy

⚠️
General Policy — No Returns
As all our products are freshly baked food items, we generally do not accept returns or exchanges once an order has been delivered or picked up. This is in accordance with standard food safety and hygiene practices.
✅
Exception — Defective or Damaged Products

We take full responsibility for the quality of every item we make. If you genuinely receive a product that is damaged, spoiled, or significantly different from what was ordered, you are eligible for a replacement or refund — subject to the conditions below.

1.
Report within 2 hours of receiving your order. Complaints raised after this window may not be entertained.
2.
Provide clear photographic or video proof of the defective product. The item must be mostly uneaten and in its original packaging.
3.
Contact us via WhatsApp with your Order ID and proof. Our team will review and respond within a few hours.
4.
Upon verification, we will offer a replacement at the earliest or a full refund via the original payment method.
